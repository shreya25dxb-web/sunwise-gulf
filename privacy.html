Privacy, in plain language.

SunWise Gulf is an educational weather, daylight and nutrition-planning prototype. We do not ask for accounts or identity information.

On your device

Sleep times, food entries, weekly outdoor-break check-ins and planner preferences are stored in your browser’s local storage, not a SunWise account. Other people who use this browser profile may see them. Clearing site storage removes them. You can export your food and sleep records from the dashboard.

Weather and barcodes

The browser requests weather and UV forecasts from Open-Meteo, and if you choose barcode lookup, product data from Open Food Facts. Those services receive their ordinary request metadata, such as an IP address, under their own policies.

Photo-based AI

Photo analysis is optional and requires ticking a consent box for each page session. Only when you press “Recognise foods” does your selected photo go through the website's secure server endpoint to Google Gemini for image recognition. The server is designed not to write the image to its database or file storage. The AI provider may process submitted information under its own policies; review Google's Gemini API terms before sharing. Do not submit photos of people, faces or personal information.

Photo suggestions do not automatically enter your diary. You must select a matching food type and enter the number of reference servings before an estimate is saved locally. Food images cannot reliably reveal brands, fortification, weights or actual vitamin D content.

For the person publishing this site

If you make this site public, you are responsible for reviewing your hosting provider's privacy controls, usage limits, accessibility, consent obligations and applicable regional data protection requirements. Add contact information and a policy reflecting any third-party analytics or changes before a public launch.
