BUILT ON SCIENCE, NOT GUESSWORK

What SunWise can — and can't — tell you.

Clear about data quality, safety, and where estimates begin.

01

Updated local forecasts

Weather, apparent temperature, humidity and sunrise/sunset come from Open-Meteo; UV forecasts come from its air-quality forecast service. Values refresh every 15 minutes while open, but forecasts are not sensor readings.

Open-Meteo docs ↗
02

Sun protection over sunbathing

At UV 3+, use shade, protective clothing and sunscreen. Sunlight through window glass doesn't generate vitamin D in skin, and UV index cannot reliably predict individual vitamin D synthesis.

WHO UV guidance ↗
03

Food amounts are estimates

Built-in serving values are from the NIH vitamin D fact sheet. Actual amounts depend on brand, fortification, recipes and portion sizes. Barcode data is community-contributed and may omit vitamin D.
